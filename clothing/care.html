Care center

Most questions never need a human. Loom Assist (chat button) runs 24/7 — same answers, instant.

How do worldwide shipping windows work?

We fulfill via partners with regional warehouses when stock allows (faster US/EU/UK). Classic global lanes run ~10–18 business days. You’ll always see an estimate before ordering and tracking after ship.

How do you keep return rates low?

Size charts on every SKU, fit callouts, fit-finder tool, size confirmation at checkout, unisex relaxed core styles, and one-size accessories. Fewer bad orders = less loss for you and us.

What if my size is wrong?

Contact care with order ID + photo within the exchange window. Prevention beats refunds — remeasure using the product chart first; many “wrong size” cases were skipped measurements.

Is customer service automated?

Yes for FAQs, size, shipping, and tracking. Urgent exceptions escalate to a human inbox. Production stack: chat bot + ticket tags + auto tracking emails.

Who ships my parcel?

Your order is packed and sent through our fulfilment partners to your delivery address. You’ll receive tracking when the carrier accepts the package.

Care for garments

Cold wash, hang dry when possible, steam not high heat for linen-blends. Longer fabric life = fewer “quality” refunds.

Fit finder

Spend a minute measuring — it saves you a return. Use a soft tape against bare skin for waist and over a light shirt for chest.

How to measure

Chest / bust

Tape around the fullest part, snug but not tight, arms relaxed. Use cm÷2.54 for inches.

Waist

Measure where trousers naturally sit — usually just above the hip bones, not the belly button flare.

Between sizes?

Oversized styles → size down one. Fitted / rib → size up one. Accessories → one size is fine.

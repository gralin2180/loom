How to measure

01

Chest & bust

Wrap a soft tape around the fullest part. Keep it level, breathe normally, and leave your arms relaxed.

02

Your waist

Measure your natural waist without pulling the tape tight. For low-rise styles, also measure where the waistband will sit.

03

A familiar favourite

Compare garment measurements with a similar piece you already love. Check whether a chart gives body or garment measurements.

What if I’m between sizes?

Check the garment’s measurements, stretch and intended fit. There is no universal rule to size up or down.

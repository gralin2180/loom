# Free store + marketing — how to sell without Meta/Google ad spend

## What you have

Premium free storefront brand **SOLA** in `/store`:

| Path | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| `store/index.html` | Home |
| `store/shop.html` | All products |
| `store/product.html?id=…` | Product page |
| `store/cart.html` | Cart + order form |
| `store/js/products.js` | Catalog + supplier search hints |
| `store/js/config.js` | Brand, WhatsApp, payment link |

### Run locally
Open `store/index.html` in a browser (double-click or Live Server).

### Host for free (public URL)
Any of these — all free static hosting:

1. **Cloudflare Pages** — drag the `store` folder
2. **Netlify Drop** — [app.netlify.com/drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop)
3. **GitHub Pages** — push repo → enable Pages on `/store`

Then paste your live URL into TikTok/IG bio.

### Go live checklist
1. Edit `store/js/config.js` → email, optional WhatsApp number, optional payment link
2. Order a sample of each product from its **real** supplier URL before marketing hard
3. Product photos must come from that listing or your sample — not random stock photos
4. When someone orders: open the downloaded JSON → order from **that** supplier URL → ship to the customer address

### Payments (still free to set up; fees only on sale)
- **Stripe Payment Links** or **PayPal.me** → put URL in `paymentLink`
- Checkout still collects shipping info and downloads a fulfill file either way

## Free alternatives to Meta Ads & Google Ads

**There are no true “free Meta/Google ads.”**  
Meta and Google charge for paid reach. Free alternatives = **organic / owned / marketplace** distribution.

### Tier S — do these first (closest thing to free “ads”)

| Channel | Why it works | Daily habit |
|---------|----------------|-------------|
| **TikTok organic** | Best discovery for impulse products | 1–3 short videos / day |
| **Instagram Reels** | Same creatives, second audience | Post same clip same day |
| **YouTube Shorts** | Longer shelf life than Reels | Upload best TikToks |
| **Pinterest** | Visual search; pins live for *months* | 5–15 product pins / week |
| **CapCut** | Free video editor for all of the above | Batch film weekly |

### Tier A — free traffic with built-in buyers

| Channel | Cost | Note |
|---------|------|------|
| **Facebook Marketplace** | Free listing | Local / impulse; no full “store” needed |
| **eBay** | Free/low listing; fee on sale | Built-in search traffic |
| **Etsy** | Listing fees | Best for “handmade look” / curated |
| **TikTok Shop** (where available) | Platform fees | Combine with organic TikTok |
| **Reddit** (value posts only) | Free | Soft promo only — hard sell = ban |
| **Quora / niche forums** | Free | Answer questions → link sparingly |

### Tier B — free Google surface (not Google Ads)

| Tool | What you get |
|------|----------------|
| **Google Search Console + free site** | Organic SEO over months |
| **Google Merchant Center free listings** *(needs eligible domain + product feed)* | Free Shopping-style results when you qualify — **not** the same as paid Shopping ads |
| **Google Business Profile** | Only useful with local pickup/address |

### Tier C — “ads-like” but still free time-cost

| Method | How |
|--------|-----|
| **Micro-influencers** | Gift 1 unit for a Reel (pay product only) |
| **UGC seeding** | Friends film unboxings; you repost |
| **Email list** | Collect emails on site later; free broadcast tools (Brevo free tier) |
| **WhatsApp / Telegram broadcast** | After first buyers |

## What is *not* free (but people call it free)

| Product | Reality |
|---------|---------|
| Meta Ads boosted posts | Still paid |
| Google Ads “coupon” | Temporary credit, then paid |
| TikTok Ads spark promos | Paid |
| “Click here free traffic tools” | Usually bots / scam |

Use those **only after** a product already gets organic traction.

## 14-day free marketing plan for SOLA

| Day | Action |
|-----|--------|
| 1 | Host store live; put link in TikTok + IG bio |
| 2–3 | Film Aperture Sunset Lamp (before/after wall, room tour) |
| 4–5 | Film Aurora Dome at night (ASMR lights off) |
| 6 | Film Linaria Oil Mister spray demo |
| 7 | Post 3 Pinterest pins per product |
| 8–10 | Rewrite hooks: “Stop buying ugly desk lamps…” / “POV: your room has a vibe filter” |
| 11 | Reply every comment + DM as sales |
| 12–13 | Cross-post best 3 to Reels + Shorts |
| 14 | Kill weakest product; double down on the video with most views |

**Rule:** 15 posts with ~0 sales is normal. Kill the product, not the channel.

### Hook templates (first 2 seconds)
- “I spent $9 and sold the vibe for $39…”
- “Your room is one lamp away from looking expensive.”
- “Amazon version is fine. This one photographs.”
- “Watch the light hit the wall…”

## Fulfillment flow (real dropshipping)

1. Customer orders → JSON ticket downloads  
2. You open the **exact supplier product URL** stored for that SKU  
3. Ship to **customer’s** address  
4. Message tracking to customer email/WhatsApp  

Never fulfill a different-looking product than what the site shows.

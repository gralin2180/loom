# Dropshipping Roadmap — The No-Budget, Organic Route

> Your situation: **$0 ad budget, global market, beginner.**
> Your edge: **time + content**, not money. This plan risks effort, not cash.

Open `index.html` in any browser to use the **Product Research Tracker** and **Profit Calculator** that go with this plan.

---

## The reality check (read this first)

- Dropshipping is a **real business**, not passive income. ~85%+ of stores fail because people treat it like a lottery ticket.
- With no money, you **cannot** buy your way to sales with ads. Your only lever is **making content people want to watch**.
- The skill you're actually building is **marketing** (hooks, video, offers). The "store" is the easy part.
- Expect **weeks of posting before anything hits**. Most products will flop. That's normal — you're looking for the one that pops.

---

## Phase 0 — Setup (Week 1, $0)

- [ ] Decide the format: **organic short-form video** (TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts).
- [ ] Create a dedicated account (don't use your personal one). Pick a niche-flavored handle.
- [ ] Install a free video editor (CapCut).
- [ ] Set up a free storefront later — **don't build the store yet**. Validate demand first.

## Phase 1 — Product research (Week 1–2, $0)

Use the **Tracker** in `index.html`. Add 15–25 candidate products and score each.

Where to find products:
- TikTok search: `#tiktokmademebuyit`, `#amazonfinds`, `#gadgets`
- AliExpress → "Best Selling" + "Weekly Bestsellers"
- Amazon → "Movers & Shakers" and "New Releases"
- Facebook/IG: look for products with ads running (means they're profitable for someone)

A good product:
- **Wows** in 2 seconds (visual, surprising)
- **Solves a problem** or triggers strong emotion
- Sells for **3×+ its landed cost** (check the Profit Calculator)
- Is **small, light, unbreakable** (cheap shipping, fewer refunds)
- **Isn't** on every shelf at the local store

Shortlist your **top 3** by score.

## Phase 2 — Validate with content (Week 2–5, $0)

- [ ] Pick your #1 product, mark it **Testing** in the tracker.
- [ ] You do NOT need to own it yet — use supplier photos/clips, or order 1 unit only if you truly can't make content without it.
- [ ] Post **1–3 videos per day**. Each video = a different **hook** (see below).
- [ ] Goal: get one video over ~10k views. That's your validation signal.
- [ ] If nothing lands after ~15 posts, kill it and move to product #2.

### Hook templates (the first 2 seconds decide everything)
- "This is why your [problem] keeps happening…"
- "I was today years old when I found out…"
- "Stop spending money on [expensive alternative]…"
- "POV: you finally fixed [annoying problem]"
- "Things TikTok made me buy that were actually worth it"
- Show the result FIRST, explain after.

## Phase 3 — Build the store (only after a video hits)

Once a product shows traction:
- [ ] Set up a simple store. Free/cheap options: a single **landing page**, or Shopify's trial, or a free Stan/Beacons-style link.
- [ ] One clear product, strong photos, 3–5 benefit bullets, reviews, clear price.
- [ ] Connect a supplier (AliExpress + DSers, Trendsi, etc.) so you can fulfill with a real SKU URL.
- [ ] Put your store link in bio and pin your best video.

## Phase 4 — Fulfill & scale (ongoing)

- [ ] When orders come in, place them with the supplier; they ship directly to the customer.
- [ ] Keep posting daily — organic reach is unpredictable, volume wins.
- [ ] Reinvest first profits into: better content, a second product, then (optionally) small paid ads.
- [ ] Track every product's real numbers in the calculator; kill losers, scale winners.

---

## Rules that keep you out of the 85% who fail

1. **Validate before you build.** Content first, store second.
2. **3× markup minimum.** If the math doesn't work in the calculator, don't sell it.
3. **Post daily, kill fast.** Emotion attached to a product = slow death.
4. **One product at a time.** Focus beats a cluttered store.
5. **Reinvest, don't withdraw** until you're consistently profitable.
